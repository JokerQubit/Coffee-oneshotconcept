## 2. Refractometric Extraction Mathematics

The Universal Gold Cup Extraction Formula implemented in the **Sensory Lab Engine**:

$$\text{Extraction Yield } (\%) = \frac{\text{TDS } (\%) \times \text{Brew Mass } (g)}{\text{Dry Coffee Dose } (g)}$$

Where:
- $\text{TDS}$ = Total Dissolved Solids measured via digital optical refractometer ($nD$).
- Ideal Pour-Over Target: $\text{TDS} \in [1.30\%, 1.45\%]$ with $\text{Extraction Yield} \in [19.5\%, 21.5\%]$.
- Ideal Espresso Target: $\text{TDS} \in [8.8\%, 10.2\%]$ with $\text{Extraction Yield} \in [19.8\%, 21.2\%]$.
