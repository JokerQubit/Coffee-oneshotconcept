## 2. Terroir Micro-Lot Taxonomy & Portfolio

Every coffee under the *Cuvée Reserve* label is harvested exclusively from certified micro-lots above 1,850 meters above sea level (MASL).

### A. Cuvée No. 001 — Panama Volcán Barú Geisha
- **Elevation:** 2,150 MASL
- **Terroir:** Volcanic Andesite Soil, Boquete Valley, Chiriquí.
- **Fermentation Protocol:** 120-hour anaerobic slow dry in hermetic steel chambers with nitrogen displacement.
- **Cupping Profile (SCA 94.8):** White Jasmine blossom, Bergamot zest, Meyer lemon brightness, White peach finish.
- **Sensory Texture:** Silk chiffon body with crystalline malic-phosphoric acidity balance.

### B. Cuvée No. 002 — Colombia Inmaculada Sudan Rume
- **Elevation:** 1,980 MASL
- **Terroir:** Pichindé, Valle del Cauca, cloud forest canopy.
- **Fermentation Protocol:** Lactic acid controlled fermentation with native yeast inoculation.
- **Cupping Profile (SCA 93.5):** Cardamom pod, crushed wild strawberry, 85% dark cocoa nibs, fresh lemongrass.
- **Sensory Texture:** Dense velvety mouthfeel with deep resonant sweetness.

### C. Cuvée No. 003 — Valle del Cauca Eugenioides
- **Elevation:** 1,850 MASL
- **Terroir:** Ancient Andean slope, shade grown under Inga trees.
- **Fermentation Protocol:** Natural Carbonic Maceration (CO2 pressurization at 18°C).
- **Cupping Profile (SCA 92.8):** Toasted marshmallow, pink guava nectar, lychee blossom, raw mountain honey.
- **Sensory Texture:** Ultra-sweet, syrupy, naturally low in caffeine (0.2% vs 1.2% in Arabica).

### D. Cuvée No. 004 — Huila Reserve Pink Bourbon
- **Elevation:** 2,050 MASL
- **Terroir:** San Adolfo, Huila, rich volcanic loam.
- **Fermentation Protocol:** Thermal Shock Double Washed (90°C thermal rinse followed by 12°C cold spring water shock).
- **Cupping Profile (SCA 91.2):** Pink grapefruit, red currant, raw cane turbinado sugar, sweet mandarin.
- **Sensory Texture:** Crisp sparkling tartaric acidity with a clean honeyed finish.

## 3. Convective Thermodynamics & Roasting Science

Maison Pur rejects conventional conductive drum roasting, which introduces bitter carbonization from metal contact. 

- **Thermal Medium:** 100% convective heated clean air fluid bed (modified Loring S35 core).
- **Rate of Rise (RoR):** Precisely regulated descending logarithmic curve.
- **Agtron Color Standard:** Commercial Light (Whole bean Agtron 86-88, Ground Agtron 102-105).
- **Degassing Protocol:** Rests 72 hours under positive nitrogen pressure before hermetic sealing.

## 4. Mineral Water Engineering Protocol

Water constitutes 98.6% of every drip extraction. Maison Pur prescribes the following ionic mineral formula for optimal receptor binding:

$$\text{Total Alkalinity} = 40 - 45 \text{ ppm CaCO}_3$$

$$\text{Magnesium to Calcium Ratio} (\text{Mg}^{2+} : \text{Ca}^{2+}) = 70 : 30$$
