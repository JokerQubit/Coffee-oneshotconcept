ABOuT US

Welcome to Maison Pur, a specialty coffee studio where every harvest is crafted to reflect a balance of elegance, terroir, and purpose. Our team of agronomists and roasters blends high-elevation varietals, natural micro-lot textures, and refined thermodynamic curves to create extractions that feel timeless and deeply personal.

We offer a full spectrum of sensory services — from botanical micro-lot development and micro-terroir curation to convective thermal profiling and complete tasting flight coordination.

THERMAL & BIOLOGICAL DISCIPLINE

STAGES OF WORK

The four immutable phases of our sensory architecture — from extreme altitude cherry picking to hermetic nitrogen aging.

/ 001

Altitude Selection

Only micro-lots grown above 1,850m in shade-canopied volcanic soils are approved, ensuring high cherry density and complex malic acidity.

/ 002

Bioreactor Fermentation

72 to 120 hours of sealed anaerobic microbial fermentation under monitored ambient pressure, unlocking rare floral esters and stone-fruit aromatics.

/ 003

Convective Roast

Fluid-bed thermodynamic roasting with zero conductive contact burns. We execute an ultra-light Scandinavian Agtron curve to preserve origin sugars.

/ 004

Nitrogen Hermetics

Hermetic nitrogen-flushed sealing in recyclable biopolymer canisters, arresting oxidation while allowing enzymatic degassing for peak flavor stabilization.
